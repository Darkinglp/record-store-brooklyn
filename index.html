ABOUT THE SPACE

A PLACE TO SLOW DOWN AND PRESS PLAY.

At Record Store, we believe music is meant to be experienced, shared, and enjoyed.

Our space brings together the warmth of a record collection, the comfort of a neighborhood lounge, and the creative spirit of Brooklyn.

Whether you're searching for your next favorite album, taking a break from the city, meeting friends, or looking for a place to work and unwind, you're welcome here.

THE EXPERIENCE

DAY & NIGHT AT 420 HUMBOLDT.

11 AM - 5 PM DAY LOUNGE

LAID-BACK WORK & LISTENING SPACE.

A relaxed daytime escape. Enjoy complimentary tea, free high-speed Wi-Fi, comfortable seating, and an analog atmosphere perfect to work, unwind, read, or discover great sounds.

AFTER 5 PM EVENING SOCIAL

SMOKE FRIENDLY & BYOB HANGOUT.

When the sun sets, kick back with good friends. We are 100% cannabis-friendly and BYOB (Bring Your Own Bottle). Enjoy board games, roll up, and settle in for an authentic Brooklyn evening.

+ ANALOG INTERACTION

PICK & PLAY ANY VINYL.

Browse our curated vinyl crates across all genres. Pick your favorite album, hand it to us, and we’ll put it on the turntable so the entire room hears your selection.

+ LOCAL ART GALLERY

CANVASES FOR SALE BY LOCAL ARTISTS.

Our walls are an open gallery supporting local Brooklyn creators. Original canvases and artwork are on display and available for purchase directly off the wall.
